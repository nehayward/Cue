# Release Copy — 2026.6

Marketing copy for the 2026.6 release: App Store "What's New" text and the
video / release-flow script. Source of truth for what shipped is
`ReleaseNotes.md` (user-facing) and `Changelog.md` (technical); this file
distills those into promotional copy.

## Highlights (4)

1. **Universal Search — one search, every service** *(hero)* — Search Apple
   Music, your Library, Spotify, Plex and more at once, one ranked list, now
   typo- and punctuation-tolerant.
2. **Playlist Management everywhere** — Add, create, edit, reorder, delete across
   Apple Music, Spotify, Plex, Deezer — with Undo.
3. **Multiple Homes** — Cue remembers every Sonos system and reconnects
   instantly; walk into a friend's house and it just connects.
4. **Song Previews** — Long-press or swipe a track to hear a clip, progress bar
   fills as it plays, tap to stop.

## App Store "What's New"

**Short (top of the box):**

> **One search for everything.** Search Apple Music, your library, Spotify, and
> Plex at once — one ranked list, now forgiving of typos. **Your playlists,
> everywhere.** And **Multiple Homes** so Cue reconnects the moment you're on a
> new network.

**Full:**

> **Universal Search**
> Pick up to three services in the search menu and get one combined, ranked
> list. Search now forgives typos and punctuation.
>
> **Playlist Management**
> Manage playlists on Apple Music, Spotify, Plex, and Deezer — add to several at
> once, create, reorder, remove, all with Undo (⌘Z on Mac).
>
> **Multiple Homes**
> Cue remembers every Sonos system you connect to. Switch homes with a tap, and
> Cue finds new systems (like a friend's house) automatically.
>
> **Song Previews**
> Long-press an Apple Music or Spotify track — or swipe right on it — to hear a
> quick clip. A progress bar fills as it plays; tap to stop.
>
> Plus faster Spotify, smoother sheets on iPad and Mac, and a batch of Plex fixes.

## Video Script — four beats

Each beat is one continuous screen recording, ~10 seconds.

**① Universal Search** *(hero)*
> **Screen:** Open search → service menu → check **Library** + **Apple Music** +
> **Spotify** → type "dont stop me now" → one ranked list resolves, Queen up top.
> **VO:** *"One search across every service — and it forgives your typos."*

**② Playlist Management**
> **Screen:** Song ⋯ → **Add to Playlist** → tap two playlists → **Done**. Cut to
> a playlist → drag to reorder → swipe to remove → **Undo**.
> **VO:** *"Your playlists now live everywhere — add to a few at once, reorder,
> undo anything."*

**③ Multiple Homes**
> **Screen:** Preferences ▸ Households → two homes with S1/S2 badges → tap the
> other home → speakers populate instantly.
> **VO:** *"Cue remembers every system — walk into a friend's house and it just
> connects."*

**④ Song Previews**
> **Screen:** Swipe right on a track → **Preview** → progress bar fills across the
> row → tap the row → it stops.
> **VO:** *"Not sure it's the right track? Preview it before it hits the queue."*

**Close:** *"Cue 2026.6 — every speaker, every service, every home, from one place."*
